Reddick entered this race with no finish outside the top 15, and a 97-point advantage over Hamlin. Now, Reddick is just 51pts ahead following a 35th place finish. The biggest mover in the standings was Bubba Wallace , who jumped four positions from 15th to 11th with a third-place finish at Michigan. The biggest losers were Christopher Bell and Austin Dillon , who both lost three positions, with Bell falling from seventh to tenth after a violent crash during the final stage.
