NASCAR New Hampshire Entry Lists Skip to content Photo: Nigel Kinrade Photography It’s time for NASCAR to head north and take on New Hampshire Motor Speedway. The NASCAR Cup Series will kick off the Round of 12, while the NASCAR Craftsman Truck Series will run the final race of its Round of 10. Here’s who’s entered. Cup New Hampshire Entry List The Mobil 1 301 will be held on Sunday, Sep 21, at 2 p.m. ET. It will be broadcast on USA Network. There are 36 teams entered, meaning everyone will compete on Sunday, barring any entry list changes. This also means there are no open entries this week for the first time since World Wide Technology Raceway at Gateway two weeks ago. Truck New Hampshire Entry List The trucks will contest the Team EJP 175 on Saturday, Sep 20, at noon ET on FOX Sports 1. There are 36 trucks listed, so everyone will make the field, unless there are entry list changes. Jayson Alexander is back in the No. 02 for Young’s Motorsports, and Mason Massey is in the team’s part-time No. 20. Stephen Mallozzi will drive the Reaume Brothers Racing No. 2, while Blake Lothian and Tyler Tomassi are in the team’s Nos. 22 and 33, respectively. It’ll be Lothian’s first start of the season. Patrick Emerling will make his third truck start of the season in the No. 7 for Spire Motorsports. Christian Eckes and the No. 16 for McAnally-Hilgemann Racing return to the track this weekend. Mike Christopher Jr. will make his series debut in Halmar Friesen Racing’s No. 62. Derek White and MBM Motorsports are back with the No. 69. The No. 97 for CR7 Motorsports with Carson Kvapil as the driver has since withdrawn from the event.
