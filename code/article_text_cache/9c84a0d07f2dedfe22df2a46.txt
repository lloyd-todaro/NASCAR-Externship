How to watch NASCAR Coke 600 weekend: Full schedule, weather, start time, TV Skip to main content NASCAR Cup Charlotte How to watch NASCAR Coke 600 weekend: Full schedule, weather, start time, TV Prime takes over for NASCAR's longest race in a packed triple-header weekend at Charlotte Motor Speedway Nick DeGroot Edited: May 24, 2026, 4:32 AM Copy link Share on Facebook Share on Twitter Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on Pinterest Share on Viber Add as a preferred source Chase Briscoe, Joe Gibbs Racing Toyota Photo by: Logan Riely / Getty Images NASCAR is heading home for a triple-header weekend at Charlotte Motor Speedway, featuring the sport's longest race with the 67th running of the Coca-Cola 600. Ross Chastain is the defending winner of the Coke 600, driving from last to first to win in 2025. This weekend, he and teammate Connor Zilisch are attempting to complete 1,101 miles of racing between all three national divisions at Charlotte. Unfortunately, they may also have to contend with Mother Nature, as rain is a threat all weekend. Denny Hamlin enters Charlotte with the momentum after collecting the $1 million prize in the Dover All-Star Race, but 23XI Racing's Tyler Reddick is the championship leader. In this story, you can find all the information you need to watch this weekend's action at Charlotte, from the weekend schedule to where to watch, and even the full entry list. You can also find more info on the race's history by clicking here. NASCAR will be racing through grief at Charlotte after the sudden passing of legendary driver Kyle Busch, who died at the age of 41 on Thursday.
