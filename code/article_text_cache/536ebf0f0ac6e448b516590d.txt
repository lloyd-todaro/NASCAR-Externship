The 19-year-old NASCAR driver wound up breaking his collarbone while celebrating a win on the Xfinity Series at Watkins Glen. Zilisch outdueled Sam Mayer to earn his sixth Xfinity win of the season. As he attempted to get out of his car to stand on it and celebrate the victory, he suffered an ugly fall and tumble. He smacked his head on the concrete and appeared to be unconscious at one point.
