Reddick's 23XI Racing teammate Bubba Wallace had a DNF, making him the biggest loser in the points as he fell from third all the way to 11th. However, he is currently locked into a four-way tie for eighth, so it's not as bad as it looks. The biggest gainer was Josh Berry , who jumped from 32nd to 25th in points after his first top ten since the Daytona 500. Around the cutline, Joey Logano's third-place finish bumped him off the hot seat with Daniel Suarez now sitting on the bubble, just nine points clear of his Spire Motorsports teammate Michael McDowell .
