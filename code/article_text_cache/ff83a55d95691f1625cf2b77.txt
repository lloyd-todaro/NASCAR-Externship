McLeod on racing and roller coasters B.J. McLeod is living the best of both worlds with his Live Fast Motorsports team and brand-new roller coaster, the Pigeon Forge Racing Coaster, now open to the public. McLeod makes a return to The Racing Writer’s Podcast to discuss the surprise of being a competitive open team with him and Katherine Legge behind the wheel, how this will be the organization’s best season to date, NASCAR’s testing policy, and details on bringing to life the first side-by-side racing roller coaster.
