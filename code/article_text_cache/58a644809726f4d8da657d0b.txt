The series now heads to New Hampshire Motor Speedway, which returns to the playoffs for the first time since the 2017 season. Christopher Bell , who just completed a Joe Gibbs Racing sweep of the Round of 16 with his Bristol win, is also the defending winner at NHMS. Bell was also one of three drivers that got to take part in a recent Goodyear tire test at the Magic Mile, alongside Joey Logano and Ross Chastain .
