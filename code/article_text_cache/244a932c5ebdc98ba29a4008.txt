Share Facebook X ReddIt Email Print NASCAR Weekend Schedule for Martinsville Speedway By Angie Campbell March 25, 2026 1 Minute Read Facebook Twitter Instagram Youtube NASCAR Photos NASCAR Photos This weekend, the NASCAR Cup Series and O’Reilly Auto Parts Series travel to Martinsville Speedway. Saturday afternoon, the O’Reilly Auto Parts Series headlines the racing action. The Cup Series closes out the weekend on Sunday with the Cook Out 400. The Craftsman Truck Series will return to competition on April 3 at Rockingham Speedway. Denny Hamlin is the returning Cup Series race winner at Martinsville .  Hamlin also leads the Cup Series among active drivers with wins at Martinsville Speedway (2008, 2009, 2010 sweep, 2015, 2025). Austin Hill returns to defend his 2025 win in the O’Reilly Auto Parts Series. The Whelen Modified Tour will take center stage Friday evening at Martinsville with the “Virginia is for Racing Lovers 200.” Friday, March 27 12:30 p.m. & 1:10 p.m.: Whelen Modified Practice 3:15 p.m.: Whelen Modified Qualifying – *Patrick Emerling on the pole 4:30 p.m.: O’Reilly Series Practice – CW App 5:35 p.m.: O’Reilly Series Qualifying – *Allgaier on the pole after qualifying was canceled due to rain. 7: p.m.: Whelen Modified Virginia is for Racing Lovers 200 *Race postponed until Saturday at 7:30 p.m. ET. 200 Laps = 105.2 Miles – FloRacing/MRN7 p.m. ET live on @FloRacing.
