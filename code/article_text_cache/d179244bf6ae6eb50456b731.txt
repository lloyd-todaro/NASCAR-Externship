Gates and hospitality will now open at 9am ET, and the Hard Rock Bet Fanzone will open at 8:30am. Parking lots will open at 6am. Additionally, expect longer lines at the entry gates because of enhanced screening processes. Fans in attendance will still get to enjoy the pre-race performance by Pitbull and the U.S. Air Force Thunderbirds performing the flyover at the conclusion of the National Anthem.
