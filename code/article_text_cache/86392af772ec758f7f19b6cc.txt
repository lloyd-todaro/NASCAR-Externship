Video: Almirola beats Bowman in wild Xfinity photo finish at Phoenix Skip to main content Race report NASCAR O'Reilly Phoenix Video: Almirola beats Bowman in wild Xfinity photo finish at Phoenix It was a jaw-dropping end to Saturday's NASCAR Xfinity race, with Almirola winning in a contact-filled photo finish that was the second closest margin of victory in series history. Nick DeGroot Edited: Mar 9, 2025, 1:21 AM Copy link Share on Facebook Share on Twitter Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on Pinterest Share on Viber Add as a preferred source A late-race spin set up a two-lap dash to the checkered flag in Phoenix's NASCAR Xfinity Series race, and it certainly did not disappoint. Justin Allgaier was on the verge of victory, but he had to hang on for one final restart. Unfortunately for the reigning Xfinity Series champion, it did not go his way as he pushed up the track in Turn 1, getting into the side of Aric Almirola . As they both slid up, Brandon Jones went low for the lead, but at the same time, Alex Bowman sent it up the middle in a brilliant four-wide move to take the lead. Almirola chased after him and the two drivers ended up side-by-side through the final set of corners. No one was giving an inch as Almirola ran wide, slamming doors with Bowman and shoving him into the outside wall. At the line, it was just 0.045s with Almirola claiming victory over a fuming (and smoking) Bowman. Jones was third, Ryan Sieg fourth, while Allgaier fell back to fifth. Aric Almirola, Toyota Photo by: Jonathan Bachman - Getty Images Almirola and Bowman see things differently "I just knew I needed to get from there (final corner) to here (finish line) first," said Almirola in his winner's interview, celebrating his eighth career victory. "I knew I was going to use him up a little, but trying to win the race. I feel like it was warranted. I didn't think I did anything overly egregious. I just throttled up and I knew it was gonna be a drag race to the start/finish line." Bowman obviously had another view of it all and showed his displeasure to Almirola as he passed him on the cool-down lap. "I would have hoped he would have given me a lane on exit but he just exited like I wasn't there, Bowman told The CW. "He was better than us for sure...just trying to capitalize on that restart and trying to win the race and got shoved in the fence and the race car's destroyed." The finish is the second of the weekend to end in such dramatic fashion. The Friday night ARCA race came down to a one-lap run to the checkered flag, culminating with Brent Crews pushing Brenden 'Butterbean' Queen into the outside wall as they exited the final corner.
