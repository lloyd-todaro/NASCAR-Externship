This is the earliest WGI has ever appeared on the schedule, and the weather looks iffy with some rain expected. Shane van Gisbergen is the defending Cup winner at Watkins Glen, and he is hoping to secure his first win of the 2026 season this weekend. Championship leader Tyler Reddick won the only road course race held so far this season, besting SVG at the Circuit of the Americas. The Glen will look a bit different with new tire packs lining the outside of Turn 1 and the Carousel, limiting the runoff drivers can utilize throughout the weekend. In this story, you can find all the information you need to watch this weekend's action at Watkins Glen, from the weekend schedule to where to watch, and even the full entry list.
