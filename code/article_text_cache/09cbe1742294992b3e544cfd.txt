Were Roles Reversed, Joey Logano Would Have Moved Christopher Bell Skip to content Original Image from Nigel Kinrade Photography; Graphic by Kevin Nix Christopher Bell was the one holding the All-Star Race trophy and $1 million after he moved Joey Logano out of the way in the final laps. Logano initially said after the race that he would have paid Bell back had he gotten back to his bumper. He clarified his comments one day later, saying he would have done the same thing to the No. 20 car if he was second and Bell was first. How far would he have gone? Would he have moved Bell similar to his maneuver to Byron at Darlington in 2022? Frontstretch’s Kevin Nix answers this week’s question for NASCAR Mailbox on Frontstretch’s YouTube channel.
