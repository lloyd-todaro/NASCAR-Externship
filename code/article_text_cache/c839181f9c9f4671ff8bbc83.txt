That leaves six drivers to fight for the remaining two spots at Martinsville this weekend. Christopher Bell and Kyle Larson have a large margin over the elimination zone, but there is just one point between the two of them. A win by Joey Logano , Ryan Blaney , William Byron , or Chase Elliott at Martinsville will automatically eliminate whoever the unfortunate driver in fourth is at the checkered flag. All four of these drivers currently in the elimination zone have won at Martinsville before. This weekend will also feature the Round of 8 elimination races for the lower two national divisions. The weekend has already gotten underway with the NASCAR Whelen Modified season finale and a Mazda MX-5 Cup race at the historic short track.
