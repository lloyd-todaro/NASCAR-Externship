Who is racing at EchoPark Speedway? There are 38 teams entered for this weekend's Cup race with two open entires. After an early exit from the Daytona 500, BJ McLeod is back with the No. 78 team, while JJ Yeley is in the No. 44 for NY Racing Team as well. As for the lower two divisions, there are several Cup drivers in the NCTS including Kyle Busch , Ricky Stenhouse Jr . and Carson Hocevar , while Ross Chastain is the only full-time Cup driver competing in the NOAPS race.
