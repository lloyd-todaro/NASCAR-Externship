Allmendinger compares himself to Kyle Larson: "Sometimes jealousy and sadness" Skip to main content NASCAR Cup Homestead-Miami Allmendinger compares himself to Kyle Larson: "Sometimes jealousy and sadness" AJ Allmendinger says he’ll never be Kyle Larson — but admits he’s in awe watching him race. Alex Harrington Edited: Mar 24, 2025, 1:34 AM Copy link Share on Facebook Share on Twitter Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on Pinterest Share on Viber Add as a preferred source AJ Allmendinger, Kaulig Racing Chevrolet Photo by: Chris Graythen - Getty Images Kaulig Racing ’s AJ Allmendinger compared himself to 2021 NASCAR Cup Series champion Kyle Larson in the lead up to the Straight Talk Wireless 400 at Homestead-Miami Speedway. Former champion Larson took the win at Sunday's race after a challenging qualifying session. Starting the race in 14th, the driver put in a commanding drive, steadily working his way through the pack to finish first after leading 19 laps. He was followed by Alex Bowman and Bubba Wallace , who finished second and third respectively. Larson, who has previously compared himself to Formula 1 driver Max Verstappen, is a highly accomplished driver, and Allmendinger, who finished seventh today admires him greatly. Discussing how he expected to race at the Miami track, he said the following: “You can slide the car a little bit, or you can move around,” Allmendinger explained. "I think with this car, you don’t have to be pinned against the wall. And I always say I need to be better at that. I’m always constantly trying to work. Am I ever gonna be Kyle Larson? God, no — or even close to that. So I think this track, at least in October, whenever we raced it with this car, it’s like I can move around and make speed." Continuing his discussion on Larson, he revealed his thoughts on what it's like watching the talented driver utilise the high side of the track, a dangerous, but fast choice on this 1.5-mile oval. “I can watch the 5 just ripping up there, and it’s sometimes jealousy and sadness and then in awe of watching him do it.” From dirt ovals to road courses, Larson has earned a solid reputation as one of the best drivers in NASCAR. In August of 2024, the driver told FloSports that he was a "better all-round driver" than reigning F1 champion Verstappen. Kyle Larson, Hendrick Motorsports Chevrolet, Bubba Wallace, 23XI Racing Toyota Photo by: Chris Graythen - Getty Images “There’s no way he can get into a sprint car and win the Knoxville Nationals," said the driver. "There’s no way he can go win the Chili Bowl. There’s no way he can go win a Cup race at Bristol. “…That’s what gives me ease and confidence that, like, I know I’m better than him. Maybe not in an open-wheel IndyCar or Formula 1 car, but that’s one discipline. I think I would beat him in everything else. You can quote that.” The Red Bull driver was quick to respond: "We should not have that debate because everyone is good in their own right. I wouldn't be good in dirt. Maybe if I practice... I don't know. I think at the end of the day, a good racing driver picks up things quite quickly."
