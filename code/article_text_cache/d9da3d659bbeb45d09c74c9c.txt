NASCAR Xfinity playoff standings ahead of Charlotte elimination race Skip to main content NASCAR O'Reilly Kansas NASCAR Xfinity playoff standings ahead of Charlotte elimination race Three drivers have secured a spot in the Round of 8, but the battle around the cutline will be wild next weekend Nick DeGroot Edited: Sep 28, 2025, 1:41 AM Copy link Share on Facebook Share on Twitter Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on Pinterest Share on Viber Add as a preferred source Connor Zilisch, JR Motorsports Chevrolet, Brandon Jones, Joe Gibbs Racing Toyota Photo by: Sean Gardner / Getty Images Brandon Jones locked himself into the Round of 8 with a big win Saturday at Kansas Speedway, but he wasn't the only one who clinched a spot in the penultimate round. JR Motorsports' Connor Zilisch and Justin Allgaier have both scored enough points to clinch a spot one week early. The battle around the cutline will be intense in next week's elimination race at the Charlotte Motor Speedway ROVAL. Jesse Love is on the bubble with just five points between himself and the drop zone. There are three drivers within single-digit points of him, beginning with Nick Sanchez at -5pts, then Austin Hill at -7pts, and Harrison Burton at -8pts.
