It’s been a busy offseason for Childers and his group as they prepare for a new NASCAR Cup Series season. Childers revealed they have done so much work and changed so many things, it’s as if they are starting over. He sat down on The Racing Writer’s Podcast to discuss that and much more ahead of this weekend’s Clash at Bowman Gray Stadium.
