The official 2025 NASCAR Cup playoff grid Skip to main content NASCAR Cup Daytona II The official 2025 NASCAR Cup playoff grid after points reset The playoff grid for the 2025 NASCAR Cup Series championship battle is set Nick DeGroot Edited: Aug 31, 2025, 4:45 PM Copy link Share on Facebook Share on Twitter Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on Pinterest Share on Viber Add as a preferred source Jeff Gordon, Rick Hendrick, William Byron, Jeff Andrews, Hendrick Motorsports Photo by: Chris Graythen - Getty Images An intense race at Daytona decided the final two spots in the NASCAR Cup playoff grid, but the points situation was settled by end of Stage 1, with Tyler Reddick locking himself in after Alex Bowman wrecked out of the race. Reddick also crashed, but he was able to continue on with his wounded No. 45 car. Bowman had to sweat for the rest of the race as the 20 drivers facing a must-win tried to position themselves for a dramatic victory in the regular season finale. There were several of those cars in a perfect spot to do just that, but in the end, Ryan Blaney prevailed in a stunning four-wide photo finish, surrounding by five drivers facing a must-win. Daniel Suarez was second, Justin Haly 3rd, Cole Custer fourth, and Erik Jones fifth -- none of which had won yet this year. As a result of Blaney's triumph, Bowman secured the 16th and final playoff spot. The playoffs get underway at Darlington, where Denny Hamlin earned pole position. 2025 NASCAR Cup playoff grid after reset Pos.
