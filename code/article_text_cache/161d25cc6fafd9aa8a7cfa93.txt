Official race results: NASCAR Cup 2026 at Texas Motor Speedway Skip to main content Results NASCAR Cup Texas Official race results: NASCAR Cup 2026 at Texas Motor Speedway Chase Elliott is back in Victory Lane for the second time this year Nick DeGroot Edited: May 4, 2026, 12:16 AM Copy link Share on Facebook Share on Twitter Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on Pinterest Share on Viber Add as a preferred source Chase Elliott, Hendrick Motorsports Photo by: Logan Riely / Getty Images Chase Elliott led 87 of 267 laps (more than any other driver) to win Sunday's NASCAR Cup Series race at Texas Motor Speedway. He beat Denny Hamlin and Alex Bowman in a four-lap sprint to the checkered flag. 11 different drivers led laps during the race, which featured several incidents as well. In the end, 20 cars finished on the lead lap and four drivers (Heim, Gibbs, Logano, Bell) recorded DNFs. After locking out the front row in qualifying, the Spire duo of Daniel Suarez and Carson Hocevar also secured a double top ten for the team in sixth and seventh, respectively. Erik Jones won Stage 1 after staying out, while Elliott won Stage 2.
