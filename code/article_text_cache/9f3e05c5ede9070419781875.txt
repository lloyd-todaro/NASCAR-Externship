The 1.5-mile speedway is one of the last tracks that the Trackhouse Racing driver hasn’t had a start on prior to this weekend. On Friday, the 19-year-old made a return to the No. 1 JR Motorsports Registix Chevrolet for practice and qualifying under cloudy skies. Zilisch had a strong qualifying effort of fourth heading into Saturday’s race. When Saturday rolled around, there were completely different track conditions with more sunshine and temperatures in the 70s, a complete change from the day before. As the green flag flew for the 200-lap race, Zilisch was a contender through the majority of the race. He battled for the first stage win, but had to settle for second to his teammate and Stage 1 winner, Justin Allgaier. In the second stage, Zilisch would be out front, taking the lead at Lap 57, leading through Lap 93, and winning the stage.
