Austin Cindric on Team Penske Daytona plan with Ryan Blaney’s points push in focus Skip to main content NASCAR Cup Daytona II Austin Cindric on Team Penske Daytona plan with Ryan Blaney’s points push in focus Team Penske’s Austin Cindric says the Daytona regular-season finale will create a “super interesting” mix of priorities Lydia Mee Published: Aug 22, 2025, 9:31 PM Copy link Share on Facebook Share on Twitter Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on Pinterest Share on Viber Add as a preferred source Team Penske driver Austin Cindric has discussed the "super interesting dynamic" expected at the Coke Zero Sugar 400 at Daytona International Speedway, as teams head into the race with varying priorities. The Coke Zero Sugar 400 marks the end of the NASCAR Cup regular season. While William Byron has already clinched the regular season championship title, the playoff picture is yet to be finalised. Cindric and his Team Penske team-mates, Ryan Blaney and Joey Logano , have already secured their spots in the playoffs, but the 26-year-old driver has admitted that it is still important to understand the priorities of the other teams throughout the field as they potentially work together to secure one of the last playoff positions. “I think as far as what’s best for the company, anyway we can get playoff points, it’s pretty simple and no different than any other week," Cindric explained regarding Team Penske's priorities this weekend. "If one of us can win stages and win races, that only helps all of us into the playoffs – all four of us [Ford drivers] – but, otherwise, I think the only other main priority is Ryan’s got a great chance to get up to second in points, which is obviously a ton of playoff points, whereas Joey and I don’t really have that opportunity. "I’m not saying the priority will be Ryan, but if we can get Ryan points, points don’t really matter for me regular season-wise, so I think that’s really the only thing that falls in the category of what else is out there, but I think, otherwise, if we can get one of us a win, even having another Ford in the playoffs is a big deal, so we’ll do our best to try and contribute to that cause.” Austin Cindric, Team Penske Ford Photo by: Sean Gardner / Getty Images He added: “I definitely make sure as a competitor that I keep myself as educated as possible on what everybody’s priorities are because you have to understand the decision-making of those around you to make your own decisions, so there’s definitely a ton of priorities throughout the field. "Who cares about points and who doesn’t and who just needs to win. Who has the experience enough to win versus who doesn’t. It’s a super interesting dynamic as a competitor to kind of overview this, whereas maybe the Daytona 500 is a little simpler. You just know everybody wants to win and that’s it.” We want your opinion!
