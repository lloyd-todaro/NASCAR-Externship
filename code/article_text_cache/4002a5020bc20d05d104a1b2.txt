Johnson gives insight into his new role, the progress of the organization, and why the belief is that self-sufficiency (not having an alliance partner) is the way to go in the long run. He also offers thoughts on understanding the Open Exemption Provisional, following the antitrust lawsuit against NASCAR, expanding Legacy Motor Club, Indianapolis 500 rumors and much more.
