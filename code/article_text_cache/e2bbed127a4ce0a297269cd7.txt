What is the weather for the 2025 Daytona 500 at Daytona International Speedway? MOTORSPORTS What is the weather for the 2025 Daytona 500 at Daytona International Speedway? Michelle R. Martinelli USA TODAY Sports Feb. 13, 2025, 4:51 p.m. ET The 2025 NASCAR Cup Series season kicks off Sunday with the Daytona 500 — the biggest race on the schedule . But, as it often happens when NASCAR is at Daytona, Mother Nature may have an impact on race day and make her presence felt. So what's the weather for the 2025 Daytona 500 at Daytona International Speedway? The green flag is set to fly at 2:30 p.m. ET on Fox. According to The Weather Channel , Sunday's forecast for Daytona Beach, Florida is a high of 82 degrees with a 40 percent chance of rain and 20- to 30-mile-an-hour winds: "Windy with showers developing later in the day." Rain could impact the second half of the race, if not the whole thing.
