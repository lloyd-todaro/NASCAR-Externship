He will be seeking redemption after making it all the way to the Championship 4 in both 2023 and 204, and then falling short of the crown. Jake Garcia secured the tenth and final spot in the playoffs, out-running and out-scoring Ben Rhodes at Richmond Raceway in order to keep himself inside the playoffs.
