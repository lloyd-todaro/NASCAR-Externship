The Darlington NASCAR Cup Series race is under threat of rain. Lydia Mee Edited: Apr 19, 2025, 4:31 PM Copy link Share on Facebook Share on Twitter Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on Pinterest Share on Viber Add as a preferred source Tyler Reddick, 23XI Racing Toyota Photo by: James Gilbert / Getty Images NASCAR heads to Darlington Raceway for the next rounds of the Cup and Xfinity Series. Although the race weekend is set to get underway with dry yet humid conditions, the weather could take a turn for the Cup Series Goodyear 400 on Sunday when the likelihood of rain increases significantly. The Darlington race weekend comes on the back of the Cup, Xfinity, and Craftsman Truck Series at Martinsville. Joe Gibbs Racing driver Denny Hamlin secured the victory in the Cup Series followed by Christopher Bell and Bubba Wallace . Austin Hill celebrated on Victory Lane in the Xfinity Series, and Daniel Hemric clinched the win in the Craftsman Truck Series. As it's just the Cup and Xfinity Series taking place in Darlington, the on-track action kicks off on Saturday, 5 April. NASCAR at Darlington: Current weather report Saturday, 5 April 10:05 a.m. ET: NASCAR Xfinity Series - Practice 11:10 a.m. ET: NASCAR Xfinity Series - Qualifying 12:35 p.m. ET: NASCAR Cup Series - Practice 1:40 p.m. ET: NASCAR Cup Series - Qualifying 3:30 p.m. ET: NASCAR Xfinity Series - Sport Clips Haircuts VFW Help A Hero 200 According to Weather.com, Saturday will see highs of 31°C with a humidity of 61%. There's a very low risk of rain, currently sat at 8%, and winds SSW are expected between 10-15 mph. The sun will set at 19:44 local time. Sunday, 6 April 3:00 p.m. ET: NASCAR Cup Series - Goodyear 400 Temperatures will drop slightly to 30°C, and the humidity will remain at 61%. However, the threat of rain significantly increases with a 72% chance of a downpour. Sunset is expected at 19:44 local time.
