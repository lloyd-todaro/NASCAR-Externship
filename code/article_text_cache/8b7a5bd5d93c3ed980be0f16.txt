(Photo by James Gilbert/Getty Images) Share Facebook X ReddIt Email Print NASCAR Weekend Schedule – Circuit of the Americas and St. Petersburg By Angie Campbell February 26, 2026 1 Minute Read Facebook Twitter Instagram Youtube NASCAR Photos NASCAR Photos This weekend, the NASCAR Cup Series and O’Reilly Auto Parts Series travel to Circuit of the Americas (COTA). The NASCAR Craftsman Truck Series will compete at the Grand Prix of St. Petersburg. There have been five Cup Series races at the 2.40-mile road course track with five different winners. Chase Elliott won the inaugural race in 2021, followed by Ross Chastain (2022), Tyler Reddick (2023) and William Byron (2024). Christopher Bell is the returning Cup Series race winner at Circuit of the Americas.
