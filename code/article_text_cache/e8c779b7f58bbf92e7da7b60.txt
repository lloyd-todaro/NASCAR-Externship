Following a somber race weekend in Charlotte after the tragic passing of Kyle Busch , the NASCAR season continues on, and Nashville will host all three national divisions. Daniel Suarez earned a surprise win in last weekend's Coca-Cola 600, taking the lead with a two-tire call and holding the top spot until rain ended it 27 laps short of the scheduled distance. Ryan Blaney is the defending winner of the Nashville Cup race. Ryan Preece , who lost an appeal of his Texas point penalty/fine, now sits on the Chase bubble in 16th place, 15 points ahead of Austin Cindric . In this story, you can find all the information you need to watch this weekend's action at Nashville, from the weekend schedule to where to watch, and even the full entry list. Thankfully, even with some inclement weather expected for Nashville, it won't be that bad. There is at least a 50% chance of rain for most of the weekend, with thunderstorms passing through the region intermittently. Sunday is the best day of the weekend, with only a passing storm, while Friday is likely to feature some delays. It will be a warm weekend with a high of 87 degrees (Fahrenheit) on Friday, 79 on Saturday, and 81 on Sunday. Who is competing in the Cracker Barrel 400? There are 36 chartered entries and two open entries. Chad Finchum will drive the No. 66 while Corey Heim is back in the No. 67 in a fourth 23XI entry. Austin Hill will continue to drive the renumbered No. 33 RCR Chevy following the death of Kyle Busch.
