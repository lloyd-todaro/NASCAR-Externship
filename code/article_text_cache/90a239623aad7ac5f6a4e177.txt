NASCAR Iowa Entry Lists Skip to content (Photo: Jonathan Bachman/Getty Images) The NASCAR Cup Series and NASCAR Xfinity Series hit up Iowa Speedway this weekend for both circuits’ lone visit to the track in 2025. Here’s who’s entered. Cup Iowa Entry List The Iowa Corn 350 Powered by Ethanol is on Sunday, Aug. 3, at 3:30 p.m. ET. It will be broadcast on USA. There are 37 entries listed, meaning everyone will run on Sunday, unless there are entry list changes. Joey Gase will make his first Cup start of the year in the No. 66 for Garage 66. Xfinity Iowa Entry List The Xfinity Series will run the Hy-Vee Perks 250 on Saturday, Aug. 2, at 4:30 p.m. ET. The CW will handle the broadcast. For Ty Dillon, NASCAR’s In-Season Challenge Means More than Money Thirty-eight drivers are entered, meaning everyone will make the full field for Saturday’s action, barring any entry list changes. Patrick Emerling is back in the No. 07 for SS-Green Light Racing this week. The JR Motorsports No. 9 will be driven by Ross Chastain . Corey Day is back to run the No. 17 for Hendrick Motorsports. Justin Bonsignore will drive the No. 19 for Joe Gibbs Racing. Tyler Tomassi will make his first career Xfinity start in the No. 35 for Joey Gase Motorsports. It’s Thomas Annunziata ‘s turn to drive the No. 70 for Cope Family Racing this week.
