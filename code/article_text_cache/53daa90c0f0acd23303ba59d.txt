I think we overheated it a little bit.” Elliott had a laugh over it too. “When I left the house this week, I don't think I had me on the back of another man's scooter on my bingo card, but here we are,” Elliott said. “And you know what? I was going to walk and my man offered me a ride and off we went. “So fortunately he had a secondary scooter here and he was kind enough to let me have that one on the way back tonight so we don't have to do that again, which is alright with me.” Photos from San Diego - Friday San Diego - Friday, in photos San Diego - Friday, in photos San Diego - Friday, in photos San Diego - Friday, in photos San Diego - Friday, in photos San Diego - Friday, in photos San Diego - Friday, in photos San Diego - Friday, in photos San Diego - Friday, in photos San Diego - Friday, in photos San Diego - Friday, in photos San Diego - Friday, in photos San Diego - Friday, in photos San Diego - Friday, in photos San Diego - Friday, in photos San Diego - Friday, in photos San Diego - Friday, in photos San Diego - Friday, in photos San Diego - Friday, in photos San Diego - Friday, in photos San Diego - Friday, in photos San Diego - Friday, in photos San Diego - Friday, in photos San Diego - Friday, in photos San Diego - Friday, in photos San Diego - Friday, in photos San Diego - Friday, in photos San Diego - Friday, in photos San Diego - Friday, in photos San Diego - Friday, in photos San Diego - Friday, in photos San Diego - Friday, in photos San Diego - Friday, in photos San Diego - Friday, in photos San Diego - Friday, in photos San Diego - Friday, in photos San Diego - Friday, in photos San Diego - Friday, in photos San Diego - Friday, in photos San Diego - Friday, in photos San Diego - Friday, in photos San Diego - Friday, in photos San Diego - Friday, in photos San Diego - Friday, in photos San Diego - Friday, in photos San Diego - Friday, in photos San Diego - Friday, in photos San Diego - Friday, in photos San Diego - Friday, in photos San Diego - Friday, in photos NASCAR Cup 50 We want your opinion!
