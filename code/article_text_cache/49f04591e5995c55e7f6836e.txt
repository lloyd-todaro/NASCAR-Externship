When Denny Hamlin tangled with the car he owns, driven by Bubba Wallace , it opened the door for Elliott sneak through and earn his 21st career win. NASCAR now heads to the Charlotte Motor Speedway Roval for the final road course race of the 2025 season as part of a tripleheader weekend. Defending series champion Joey Lognao is on the bubble, 13 points above Ross Chastain . Bubba Wallace is 26 points back, Tyler Reddick 29 points behind, and Austin Cindric 48 points adrift. Kyle Larson is the defending winner at the Roval, but all eyes will be on Shane van Gisbergen as he attempts to win a fifth consecutive road course race this year.
