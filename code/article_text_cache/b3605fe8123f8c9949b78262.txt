Here's your quick guide to making sure you don't miss a single lap. (All times Eastern.) NTT INDYCAR SERIES Patches of cloud and a very, very slight chance of rain await the IndyCar Series field at IMS on Saturday. For Chevrolet teams, the weekend is about breaking what has so far been a Honda lockout of victory lane this season, while everyone who doesn't drive Alex Palou's No. 10 Chip Ganassi Racing car will be looking for a way to end the reigning champion's hot streak. Where is this weekend's IndyCar race? The Sonsio GP is held on the Indianapolis Motor Speedway road course. What time is this weekend's IndyCar race? Saturday, 4:30pm What channel is the IndyCar race on? FOX Where can I stream this weekend's IndyCar race? Several streaming services carry FOX, including YouTubeTV, SlingTV and others. See your streaming provider for more details. NASCAR CUP SERIES Last May's Kansas race found its way into the record books when Kyle Larson beat Chris Buescher by 0.001s – the closest finish in Cup Series history. Larson heads into this weekend among the favorites, although he's pointing to the Toyotas as the cars to beat. Where is this weekend's NASCAR race? The Adventhealth 400 is held at Kansas Speedway (1.5 miles) in Kansas City, Kan. What time is this weekend's NASCAR qualifying session? Saturday, 4pm. What channel is NASCAR qualifying on? Prime Video. What time is this weekend's NASCAR race? Sunday, 3:00pm What Channel is this weekend's NASCAR race on? FS1 Where can I stream this NASCAR race? Max. IMSA WEATHERTECH SPORTSCAR SERIES After sitting out the last round at Long Beach, the GTD PRO class rejoins GTP and GTD under what are expected to be cool and clear skies at Laguna Seca. Porsche Penske Motorsport's No. 7 car comes into the weekend looking to make it four wins from four races, and as if it needed a further confidence boost, PPM won here last year as well (albeit with the No. 6 sister car). It's a different story for the GT field, with neither class having yet produced a repeat winner this year. Where is this weekend's IMSA race? The Monterey Sportscar Championship is at WeatherTech Raceway Laguna Seca. What time is this weekend's IMSA qualifying session? Saturday, 6:10pm What channel is IMSA qualifying on? Peacock. What time is this weekend's IMSA race? Sunday, 3:00pm What channel is this weekend's IMSA race on? NBC Where can I stream this race? Peacock FIA WORLD ENDURANCE CHAMPIONSHIP The third round of the series at picturesque Spa-Francorchamps. Ferrari dominated qualifying with its 499 Hypercars, Antonio Fuoco taking pole, but can they maintain that margin over a six-hour race on this demanding circuit? Where is this weekend's WEC race? The TotalEnergies 6 Hours of Spa is at the Spa-Francorchamps grand prix circuit in Spa, Belgium. What time is this weekend's WEC race? Saturday, 8:00am Where can I stream this weekend's WEC race? The FIA WEC app and Max.
