He was followed by Brad Keselowski in second, a driver who was hoping to end a two-year win drought, but had to settle for two stage wins and a runner-up finish. Ryan Blaney fought back from a loose wheel to finish third, Carson Hocevar led the way for Chevrolet in fourth, and Austin Cindric finished fifth. At the checkered flag, 36 of the 37 starters were still running, with only Timmy Hill recording a DNF.
