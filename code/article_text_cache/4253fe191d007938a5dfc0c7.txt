Texas Motor Speedway will host all three national divisions this weekend, with the Cup, O'Reilly, and Craftsman Truck Series. Tyler Reddick continues to hold a commanding lead in the championship, 110 points clear of Denny Hamlin. Joey Logano is the defending winner at Texas -- his most recent victory in the Cup Series. In this story, you can find all the information you need to watch this weekend's action at Texas, from the weekend schedule to where to watch, and even the full entry list.
