Along with the top division, the NASCAR Craftsman Truck Series and ARCA Menards Series will also be racing there this weekend. Denny Hamlin earned his 62nd career win last weekend at Nashville after a thrilling battle with Joe Gibbs Racing teammates Christopher Bell and Chase Brisce. Hamlin is also the defending winner at Michigan, and has a chance to tie late NASCAR legend Kyle Busch on the all-time wins list. In this story, you can find all the information you need to watch this weekend's action at Michigan, from the weekend schedule to where to watch, and even the full entry list.
