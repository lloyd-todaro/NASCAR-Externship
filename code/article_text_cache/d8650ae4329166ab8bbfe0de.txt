How to watch the 2025 NASCAR Bristol Night Race on TV Skip to main content Preview NASCAR Cup Bristol II How to watch 2025 NASCAR Bristol Night Race: Weekend schedule, start time, TV NASCAR Cup, Xfinity, Truck, and even ARCA are all racing at Bristol Motor Speedway in a packed weekend of racing ahead Nick DeGroot Edited: Sep 13, 2025, 8:20 PM Copy link Share on Facebook Share on Twitter Share on WhatsApp Share on Linkedin Share on Pinterest Share on Viber Add as a preferred source Kyle Larson leads the field Photo by: Jeffrey  Vest / Icon Sportswire via Getty Images The first round of eliminations is just ahead in the 2025 NASCAR Cup Series playoff fight. After 500 laps of racing at Bristol, 12 drivers will remain in contention for the championship. Austin Cindric sits on the hot seat, while the following drivers are below the cutline: Austin Dillon (-11pts), Shane van Gisbergen (-15pts), Alex Bowman (-35pts), and Josh Berry (-45pts). Both Chase Briscoe and Denny Hamlin are locked into the next round after their victories at Darlington and Gateway, respectively. Kyle Larson , who has a comfortable points cushion, enters Bristol as the defending winner of the late-summer night race. News of the week This week, NASCAR gave a stern warning after Trackhouse Racing's playoff contenders drove through the infield grass following the race at Gateway, possibly in attempt to add extra weight. Additionally, there has been a pit crew swap between the No. 48 Hendrick Motorsports Chevrolet and the No. 77 Spire Motorsports Chevrolet, effective immediately. NASCAR is bringing a softer right-side tire to Bristol, which could increase tire falloff , but there are other factors that leave many unknowns for the weekend ahead.
