Around the cut-line, Austin Cindric has moved back inside the top 16, and is now two points ahead of Ryan Preece , who recorded a DNF at Nashville. Joey Logano is the next-closest, nine points back. The biggest mover in the points this week is Ricky Stenhouse Jr ., who jumped three positions from 26th to 23rd with a top five finish . The biggest losers are AJ Allmendinger (19th to 22nd) and Ross Chastain (23rd to 26th), as each dropped three positions in the standings. Note: Kyle Busch scored 217 points during the 2026 season before his tragic passing.
