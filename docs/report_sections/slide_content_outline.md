## Slide 2: Executive Summary

**The Bottom Line**

- Progressive delivers the highest total visibility of the five sponsors analyzed (477.6 points, rank #1 of 5)
- Cheddar's Scratch Kitchen delivers the best efficiency: 39.9 visibility points per $M invested, about 1.5x Progressive's rate
- Race performance is 40% of the score, but Cheddar's shows that media visibility can outweigh on-track results at a fraction of the cost

**Primary Recommendation**: Cheddar's Scratch Kitchen, the best efficiency in the group and #2 in total visibility on the smallest budget of the five

## Slide 3: Project Approach

**How We Measured Sponsorship Visibility**

[Visual: Data Sources --> Scoring Model --> Rankings]

- Analyzed 5 primary sponsors across 53 tracked races from the 2025 season and 2026 season-to-date (each sponsor's own race count ranges from 11 to 29)
- Combined race results, social media, and news coverage into one composite score
- Weighted as race performance (40%), a PCA-weighted visibility index across Reddit, YouTube, and news (50%), and special events like wins and playoffs (10%)

**Why it matters**: NY Racing has never had a single, consistent way to compare sponsor visibility across channels. This model gives them one.

## Slide 4: Data and Limitations

**What We Measured**

| Category | Source | Note |
|----------|--------|------|
| Race Performance | Racing Reference | 53 races |
| Social Media | Reddit (Arctic Shift), YouTube Data API | Fan engagement |
| News Coverage | GDELT DOC 2.0 + BigQuery GKG, ~19 curated domains | Weighted by source tier |
| Costs | Forbes, Jayski, Speedway Media, RTR Sports, team/league press | Estimates, not disclosed contracts |

**Important caveat**: Cost estimates are approximations, not actual contract values. Cheddar's Scratch Kitchen's $4.9M figure is the least certain number in this analysis: it borrows Castrol's own sourced per-race rate rather than a disclosed number for the Cheddar's/RCR deal itself.

## Slide 5: Finding #1 - Efficiency Leadership Doesn't Track Brand Prestige

**Cheddar's Scratch Kitchen delivers the best visibility per dollar of any tracked sponsor, about 1.5x Progressive, while its budget sits close to Castrol's rather than Progressive's.**

[Visual: efficiency_comparison.png]

*So what?*: Cheddar's Scratch Kitchen is worth serious consideration as the primary target. It ranks #2 in total visibility despite the second-smallest race count in the group, at roughly a quarter of Progressive's cost.

---

## Slide 6: Finding #2 - A Playoff Race Is Worth More Than Double a Regular-Season One

**Average visibility in playoff-window races (races 27 to 36) runs 129% higher than in regular-season races, a bigger swing than the win bonus (1.79x).**

[Visual: sponsor_dashboard.png, cumulative visibility panel]

*So what?*: Treat the playoff window as a premium activation period, not just a higher-stakes version of a normal week. Content and hospitality spend should scale up specifically for that ten-race stretch.

---

## Slide 7: Finding #3 - Reddit Is the Only Channel With a Statistically Reliable Signal

**Of the three visibility channels tracked, only Reddit's relationship with race performance clears standard significance thresholds (r = -0.34, p = 0.0006, N = 97). YouTube and news trend the same direction but aren't statistically distinguishable from noise at this sample size.**

[Visual: scatter_with_regression.png]

*So what?*: Weight Reddit most heavily in any future live-monitoring dashboard, and treat YouTube and news mentions as confirmatory rather than primary signals.

## Slide 8: Recommendations

**Who Should NY Racing Pursue?**

**Primary Recommendation: Cheddar's Scratch Kitchen**
- Visibility: 195.6 points (Rank #2 of 5)
- Efficiency: 39.9 pts/$M (Rank #1 of 5)

**Alternative: Progressive**
- Best for teams that want maximum reach and the best-sourced cost estimate of the group, and are willing to pay about 3.7x Cheddar's estimated cost for it.
